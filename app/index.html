What this software does

A local, offline invoicing & accounting tool for CAs and small firms. By default your data is saved on this computer under Desktop → Invoices Utility; nothing is uploaded to the cloud. You can point that folder anywhere — including a OneDrive/Google Drive-synced folder to share one data set across devices — from Data Location above. You can run multiple companies, each with its own isolated data.

Getting started

  • Login: the app is protected by a passkey — change it from Change Passkey in the sidebar.
  • Home: the Home button in the sidebar always takes you back to the main task dashboard from anywhere in the app.
  • Companies: create and switch between firms; each keeps separate clients, invoices, vouchers, reports, and its own invoice numbering series.
  • Clients: add parties with name, contact, GSTIN, and mandatory City & Pincode.

Day-to-day work

  • Tasks & Invoices: add a task (client + line items), then Generate its invoice. Primary Bills (GST) and Secondary Bills (Non-GST) use separate numbering series (switch between them from the Bills dropdown in the sidebar). Use Generate Separate to make one invoice per selected row.
  • GST breakdown: GST invoices automatically show CGST + SGST when your client is in the same state as you, or IGST when they're in a different state — detected from GSTINs (or the client's city/pincode as a fallback) and printed with the Place of Supply, per GST invoicing rules.
  • Invoice branding: your organisation logo, address, GSTIN, and bank details all appear on generated invoices automatically once set in Settings.
  • Emailing invoices: select one or more generated invoices on the dashboard and click Email to send them straight to the client's inbox with the PDF attached. Set up your SMTP host/username/password in Settings first — the Test Email Connection button there confirms it's working before you rely on it.
  • Task actions: each row keeps Generate/Re-generate, View, and Download visible; less-frequent actions — Edit, Receipt Entry, Cancel Invoice, Delete — live under the ⋮ More menu.
  • Receipt Entry / Vouchers: record payments against an invoice, or as an on-account advance. Extra money auto-adjusts to the party's other pending invoices, then becomes an advance.
  • Reports: Sales, Outstanding, Party Ledger (with a searchable client picker), Primary & Secondary Bills — view on screen or download as Excel, PDF, or Tally XML.
  • Dashboard insights: quick trend chips under the stats bar show this month's task count and revenue against last month's.
  • Auto Reminders: fully automatic — the first WhatsApp payment reminder sends 3 days after an invoice's due date, then repeats every 3 days until it's paid. Turn it off globally, per client, or per invoice any time; reminders stop the moment an invoice is marked paid. Runs on a backend schedule, so it keeps working even when the app is minimized or closed.
  • Settings: click the gear icon on the dashboard (next to the reminders bell) to edit your organisation details, addresses, bank details, logo, and email (SMTP) setup.
  • Bulk Upload: import many tasks at once from an Excel template.
  • Delete: remove a task, client, or voucher — you'll always be asked to confirm. A client with existing tasks can't be deleted until its tasks are removed; deleting a voucher reverses the payment it applied. Note that deleting an already-invoiced task permanently retires its invoice number — use Cancel Invoice instead if you want the gap explained in your records.
  • Dark mode: toggle from the top of the sidebar.
